# LeadFlow

**Purpose:**
A simple conversational lead-collection backend that talks to users, understands what type of lead they are, collects the important details, and stores them in a structured way.

### Lead Types

* **HR →** Job/hiring lead
* **Applicant →** Candidate/job lead
* **Company →** Business lead
* **Customer →** Customer enquiry

### Flow

```text
User
 ↓
Chatbot
 ↓
Identify user type
 ↓
Ask relevant questions
 ↓
Collect & store answers
 ↓
Create Lead
```

### Technology

```text
React Native App
        ↓
Node.js + Express
        ↓
JavaScript Lead/Conversation Logic
        ↓
Database
```

The goal is to make it feel like a **simple WhatsApp-style conversation**, rather than a traditional form.
